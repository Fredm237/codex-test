# Phase 10C — BUY/WAIT V2 historical backtest

## Qualification boundary

The gate is a deterministic temporal holdout across six verticals, three locales
and four independent seeds. The engine receives only the historical prefix. A
future horizon is retained exclusively by the evaluation oracle and is never
part of `BuyWaitRequest`.

The action set is intentionally narrow:

- `BUY_NOW` only for a materially low current price;
- `WAIT` only for a materially high current price;
- `ABSTAIN` for neutral prices or any missing support, confidence or backtest
  identity.

## Ratified engineering gates

- at least 3,600 independently generated actionable cases;
- action accuracy 1.0 and Wilson lower bound at least 0.995;
- zero wrong-direction actions;
- zero unsupported actions;
- zero future leakage;
- complete evidence and claims for every action.

The report is generated by `python -m quality_lab.buy_wait` from
`quality/buy-wait-v2-manifest.json` and is published as a CI artifact.

## Governance

The gate is `AUTONOMOUS_QUALITY_LAB`. External human ground truth remains
`NO_EXTERNAL_HUMAN_GROUND_TRUTH / NON_BLOCKING`; subjective quality is
`NOT_INDEPENDENTLY_VALIDATED`. This qualification proves deterministic,
fail-closed historical policy behaviour. It does not claim a forecast of future
prices or independent commercial validation.
